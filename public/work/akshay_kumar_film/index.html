Celebrity-led campaign film

JK Maxx Paints x Akshay Kumar

Introduced Akshay Kumar as JK Maxx Paints’ brand face and launched the “Maxx Karo, Relax Karo” campaign platform.

The brief

JK Maxx Paints was bringing Akshay Kumar on as its brand face. The campaign had to introduce him while also making the brand name memorable and communicating its complete painting solution.

The idea

As a team, we explored multiple creative routes before landing on a simple recurring device: Akshay answers almost every question with “JK Maxx.”

What begins as a running joke is later explained through a reveal—when it comes to painting his home, every recommendation, from putty and primer to paint, leads back to JK Maxx. This allowed the film to introduce Akshay, communicate the product range and build brand recall through one memorable idea.

My role

I was involved in the ideation and concept-development stage. Alongside the creative and production team, I helped unpack the brief, explore different routes and shape the chosen idea around celebrity integration and brand recall. I also worked with the wider team and client as the concept moved towards execution.

Outcome

A humour-led celebrity film that gave JK Maxx Paints a memorable brand-face launch and turned “JK Maxx” into the answer audiences were meant to remember.
